Artificial Intelligence

The progress of large language models has been rapid. GPT-3 demonstrated that scaling alone could unlock surprising emergent capabilities, and follow-up work on Chinchilla scaling laws showed that most large models were significantly undertrained relative to their parameter count.

More recently, Gemini introduced native multimodality, while interpretability researchers have made early progress on sparse dictionary learning to reverse-engineer what concepts individual neurons represent.

Neuroscience & Cognition

The spacing effect is one of the most robust findings in cognitive psychology: memories reviewed at expanding intervals are retained far longer than those reviewed at fixed short intervals. Dual n-back training was once thought to transfer to general fluid intelligence, though subsequent meta-analyses have been skeptical.

Statistics

The replication crisis revealed widespread failures of reproducibility across psychology, medicine, and economics. Key drivers include p-hacking and underpowered studies.
